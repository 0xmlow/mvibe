About

I'm MLow.

I'm Michael Low, an artist in New York. I make AI art, NFT collections on Ethereum and physical metal and giclee prints.

My flowers started as symbols of hope after surviving cancer. They grew into a whole universe: about 1,800 drippy collage fLOWers, surreal Transdimensional Trippers, the electric abstractions of Impermanent Loss, and NEW YORKERS, a character world of the city I love. With painter Andrés Del Vecchio I co-created THE SOFT CONSPIRACY.

My work has ridden on more than 5,000 NYC taxis with Somo and Art Crush Gallery. I'm an Adobe Firefly Creative Partner Ambassador and work with FLORA, Dreamina and Wide World AI. NYU BA, magna cum laude, and NYU Wagner MPA.
